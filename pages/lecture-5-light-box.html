Physics · The Physics Lectures at UWC · Unit C · Lesson 5

Wave Behaviour & Light, Part II: Light Box

The same reflection rule, shown twice: once as a wavefront off a barrier in the ripple tank, once as a ray off a mirror. Then one open question, left without a formula.

Demonstration
01

Setup

Part II follows the ripple tank immediately, on the same day. I leave the ripple tank set up and put the light box and optical set next to it, mirrors only, no lenses yet.

02

What Happens

I show reflection twice, side by side. First a straight wavefront reflects from a flat barrier in the tank; then a ray reflects from a mirror in the light box. I am not simply saying that light is like a water wave. Students can see the same reflection rule in two different kinds of wave. We also begin establishing that light propagates at a constant speed while it remains in a given medium. I do not explain refraction yet. I am putting this fact alongside the source-fixed frequency from Part I so the pieces are already there when the medium changes in the following lesson. At the end I leave them with one question, without a formula: why do the waves follow that path?

03

Field Notes

These two demonstrations are one conceptual setup, not two independent lessons. The ripple tank gives students a visible mechanical-wave system; the light box extends the discussion toward light. By the time we reach refraction, the explanation should not appear from nowhere. They already have the source, frequency, wavelength, and constant speed within a medium. Their job is to put those observations and principles together, not reproduce an explanation from a textbook.

Equipment

  • Light Box & Optical Set (mirrors only)
  • Ripple tank, for side-by-side comparison

Physics

Law of reflection: \(\theta_i = \theta_r\), measured from the normal. Within a given medium, light propagates at a constant speed. Together with the source-established frequency and \(v = f\lambda\), that becomes part of the reasoning students will use when light reaches a new medium.
