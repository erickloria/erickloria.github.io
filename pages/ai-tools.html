03

AI Citation Guide

Use the full student guide for citation rules, disclosure examples, and style-specific formats for generative AI use in class.

1 Tool Specification Mention the name of the AI tool and the version used.
2 Prompt Disclosure Briefly describe the prompt provided to the AI and how you used the response.
3 Date of Access Record the exact date when the information was generated or accessed.
Best Practice When in doubt, cite and briefly explain your AI use. Transparent attribution is safer than omitting a citation. Teacher instructions for a specific assignment always take precedence over the general guide.
